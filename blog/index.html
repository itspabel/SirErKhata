The Problem with Existing Tuition Apps

Most tuition management apps on the market today fall into one of two categories:

  • Enterprise school platforms (Teachmint, MySchoolWorx, Classplus) — packed with features you'll never use, priced for institutions, and almost always cloud-based. They require sign-ups, internet, and often a hefty monthly fee.1
  • Freemium tutor apps (Tutor Pro, TuitionDesk) — start free but lock essential features behind a paywall. Many still depend on an internet connection and store your student data on their servers.2

For an individual tutor, the result is the same: you either pay too much for features you don't need, or you compromise on privacy and offline access. SirErKhata takes a different approach.

Why SirErKhata Wins for Private Tutors

1. True Offline-First Architecture

Unlike TuitionDesk, TutorBird, and Teachmint — which all require a stable internet connection to sync with cloud servers — SirErKhata runs entirely on your Android device. No internet? No problem. Your student records, fee history, and attendance data are stored locally and remain fully accessible even in areas with poor connectivity.6

2. Zero Subscription Fees

Most competing apps charge recurring fees. TutorBird starts at $16.95/month, TuitionDesk's paid plans begin at ₹99–₹499/month, and enterprise platforms like Teachmint and MySchoolWorx require custom quotes that can run into thousands per year.3,4,5 SirErKhata is distributed as a free APK via GitHub Releases — no subscriptions, no hidden costs, no credit card required.7

3. Complete Privacy & Data Ownership

When you use a cloud-based app, your students' personal information — names, phone numbers, parent details, fee records — lives on someone else's server. SirErKhata flips that model: your data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export a JSON backup. You can also lock the app with biometric or PIN protection.6
